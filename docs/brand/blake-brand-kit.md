# blake. Brand Kit

## Brand Idea

**blake.** is the AI office manager for busy trade businesses.

The name is written lowercase with a period — calm, direct and product-first. Blake brings enquiries, quotes, jobs, schedules, variations and invoices into one live office.

## Core Positioning

**blake. — Your AI Office Manager**

Short pitch:

> Blake brings your entire office together — enquiries, quotes, jobs, schedules, variations and invoices.

## Name Usage

Use:

- **blake.** for the wordmark and hero branding.
- **Blake** in sentences and product UI chrome.
- **Ask Ayla** for the intelligent assistant inside Blake.
- **Ayla** when referring to the assistant by name.

Avoid:

- NeXa / Nexa (retired product name)
- Ask Blake (retired assistant label)
- BLAKE in all caps for body copy
- Rebuilding the wordmark from a font — always use the official logo artwork

## Assistant

**Ask Ayla** is the intelligent assistant inside Blake.

Tagline:

> Ask Ayla. Your intelligent assistant inside Blake.

Ayla reads live Blake records, answers operational questions and prepares actions for human approval. Ayla never guesses outside approved materials or live workspace data.
